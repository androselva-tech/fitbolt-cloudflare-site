MasterBolt – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

MasterBolt can be played without an account, and we collect only what is needed to save your progress and show ads.

This policy explains what information the MasterBolt app ("MasterBolt", "the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. MasterBolt is a word game for Android in which players solve vocabulary puzzles and run obstacle courses (package com.androselva1988.wordadventure).

By using MasterBolt you agree to this policy. If you have questions, contact us at androselva@gmail.com.

Most of what the app stores never leaves your device. An account is optional; without one, nothing about you is sent to our servers.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone recordings, payment details, or health data. The app does not ask for access to any of these.

How we use the information

We use your information only to run the game. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to build profiles of you.

  • To run the game: your progress is saved so you can continue where you left off, and your word results choose which words to practise next.

  • To sign you in and back up your progress (optional): your account lets you restore your progress on another device. When you sign in, the progress on your device and in the cloud are combined so nothing is lost.

  • To show rewarded ads: when you choose to watch an ad (to reveal a word's picture), Google AdMob uses device information to deliver the ad, count it, and prevent fraud.

  • To answer you: if you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Data storage, security and retention

Your progress stays on your device until you delete the app; account data stays until you ask us to delete it.

  • On your device: game progress is kept in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

  • In the cloud: account and cloud-save data are stored on Google's Firebase servers, which may be outside your country. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), and access rules let each signed-in player read and write only their own data.

  • How long we keep it: we keep account and cloud-save data while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and its cloud save within 30 days.

  • Ads: data collected by AdMob is kept by Google under its own retention rules (see the link above).

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we use the protections the services above provide.

Your choices and rights

You can use MasterBolt fully without an account, and you can delete your data at any time.

  • Play without an account: signing in is optional and is only offered from the Profile screen.

  • Sign out: open Profile and tap Sign out. Your progress stays on the device.

  • Reset your progress: open Profile and tap Reset progress. If you are signed in, the cloud copy is reset too.

  • Delete your account and its data: email androselva@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my MasterBolt account". We delete your account and cloud save within 30 days and confirm by email.

  • Ads: watching an ad is always your choice. To reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, open your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

  • Access or correction: you can ask us for a copy of the data linked to your account, or to correct it, at the same email address.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. We will honour these requests.

Children's privacy

MasterBolt is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Children can play without an account, in which case nothing about them leaves the device.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child created an account, email androselva@gmail.com and we will delete the account and its data.
